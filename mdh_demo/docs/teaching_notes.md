# Teaching notes

The demo uses the following MDH table:

| joint | a_(i-1) [m] | alpha_(i-1) [rad] | d_i [m] | theta_i |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.12 | q1 |
| 2 | 0.00 | +pi/2 | 0.00 | q2 |
| 3 | 0.18 | 0.00 | 0.00 | q3 |
| 4 | 0.18 | 0.00 | 0.00 | q4 |

All joint axes use `+Z`, so the sign of the GUI angle is the sign of the
corresponding MDH `theta`.
